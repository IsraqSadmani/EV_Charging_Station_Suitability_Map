    Potential Khas Land for EV Charging Stations – Dhaka City

    This map shows the results of screening 726 khas (government-owned) land plots in Dhaka City as potential sites for electric vehicle (EV) charging stations. 49 plots were found suitable and rated High, Medium or Low.

    Screening criteria

    Each plot was checked manually in ArcGIS against three criteria:

    • Minimum area: at least 4 katha (1 katha = 66.8902 m²). Smaller adjoining plots were kept where together they form a usable site.
    • Accessibility: access from a major road.
    • Vacancy: vacant or easily cleared; not built up or in restricted use.
